No Electrolux tip covers the power outage alert. The closest is FS2621 AFCI/GFCI nuisance tripping: check outlet/breaker wiring and secure neutral and ground, with no floating ground.

CHECK BEFORE REPLACING
1

3

Look for a loose service cord connection.

4

Diagnostics: hold Refrigerator Temp + Water Filter 10 s. Run the compressor, fans and sensor tests (11–14). Compressor LED blinks: 3 = undervoltage, 4 = overvoltage, 5 = low current (check fuses F1/F2), 8 = DC supply bad.

5

Inspect the machine compartment harness and the evap fan harness. A grounded evap fan motor can kill electrical operation (tip RFTT2528).

6

Call Electrolux Tech Support before replacing the boards again.
